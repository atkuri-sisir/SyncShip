The gap

No business account

Real courier APIs need a company email and a business account. Personal users have neither, so SyncShip needs nothing to set up.

The daily list

Built for the people doing the checking.

Search by tracking number, item or invoice. Filter by courier or status. When a parcel arrives, one tap on Delivered closes it.

Shadowfax rows update on their own. For other couriers, a pre-filled link opens the courier's page in one click.

How it works

Three steps for each parcel.

  1. Add the parcel

    Courier and tracking number, plus an optional note like "Phone cover from Meesho". Nothing else is required.

  2. Get its status

    Shadowfax parcels are fetched automatically when you add them. For others, open the courier's page from SyncShip and record the result.

  3. Watch the dashboard

    The charts and the "needs attention" tile show what is late, failed or still waiting.

A decision we made
Couriers lock their tracking pages against software. We chose not to break in.
Captchas

Built to stop programs from reading status.

SMS codes

Sent to the receiver's phone before details show.

Origin checks

Requests are refused unless they claim to come from the courier's own site.

Business-only APIs

Real tracking APIs need a company account and email.

Bypassing them breaks without warning and goes against the couriers' rules. So Shadowfax is automatic, and the rest is assisted until a proper tracking key is in place.

Business model Proposal

Free for people. Paid for volume.

Individuals track their own parcels for free. Businesses that send many parcels a day pay for automatic tracking on every courier, which covers the tracking service behind it.
